Once upon a time in a land not like ours, there used to be the kingdom of mages. The mages were people capable of bending the laws of nature to their will. And the most talented mage of them all in Arcania was the seventh prince. He was a timid boy with a small frame and stature, but his mind was like no other, capable of understanding complex structures in mere seconds and display his prowess with a small wooden stick. He was the pride of the kingdom.
