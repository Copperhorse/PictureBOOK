Grandpa Omar had a long white beard and wore a straw hat. Every summer he took his grandson Sami out on the quiet blue lake in a little wooden boat. Sami, a boy in a red cap, held the fishing rod very tightly. Suddenly something tugged the line, and out came a shiny silver fish. Sami clapped his hands, and Grandpa Omar laughed so loudly that the ducks flew up from the water.
