A hungry dog found a big juicy bone and trotted home with it in his mouth. On the way he crossed a narrow wooden bridge over a quiet stream. He looked down and saw another dog in the water, holding an even bigger bone. I want that one too, thought the dog, and he barked at the dog in the water. The bone dropped out of his mouth, splashed into the stream, and sank out of sight. The dog in the water was gone as well, because it had only been his own reflection. The poor dog trotted home with nothing. Be happy with what you have.
