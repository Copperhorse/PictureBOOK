A country mouse lived in a quiet field and ate simple seeds and roots. One day her cousin, the town mouse, came to visit and said, come to the city with me, and I will show you a feast. The country mouse followed her cousin to a grand house with a long dining table covered in cakes, cheese and fruit. They had just started to eat when a big gray cat leaped into the room, and the two mice ran for their lives into a tiny hole in the wall. The country mouse was shaking. She said, your food is wonderful, but I would rather eat my plain seeds in peace than a feast in fear. She went home to her quiet field and never left it again.
