All summer long, a grasshopper played his green fiddle in the sunny meadow and sang songs. A little ant marched past him every day, carrying a grain of wheat on her back to her nest underground. Why do you work so hard, asked the grasshopper, come and sing with me. I am storing food for the winter, said the ant, and you should do the same. The grasshopper laughed and played another tune. Then autumn came, and the leaves fell, and the first snow covered the meadow. The grasshopper was cold and hungry, and he had no food at all. He knocked on the ant's door, shivering. The ant gave him a warm place by the fire and a little bread, and said, next summer, remember to prepare.
