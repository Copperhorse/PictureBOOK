A shepherd boy named Tomas looked after a flock of white sheep on a hillside above his village. He was bored, so one afternoon he shouted, wolf, wolf, a wolf is chasing the sheep. The villagers dropped their tools and ran up the hill with sticks, but they found no wolf. Tomas laughed at them. A few days later he did it again, and again the villagers ran up the hill, and again there was no wolf. Then one evening a real gray wolf crept out of the forest toward the sheep. Tomas screamed, wolf, wolf, please help me. The villagers heard him, but they said, he is fooling us again, and they stayed at home. The wolf scattered the flock, and Tomas sat alone in the dark and cried. Nobody believes a liar, even when he tells the truth.
