It was a very hot day, and a thirsty crow flew for miles looking for water. At last she found a tall pitcher standing in a garden, with a little water at the very bottom. She pushed her beak inside, but the water was too far down to reach. She tried to tip the pitcher over, but it was too heavy. Then the crow looked around and saw a pile of small pebbles. She picked up one pebble and dropped it into the pitcher, and then another, and another. Slowly the water rose higher and higher. When the water reached the top, the crow drank until she was no longer thirsty. Little by little, a clever mind can solve a hard problem.
