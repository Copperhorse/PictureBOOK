One hot summer day, a hungry fox walked through a vineyard. High up on a vine hung a bunch of ripe purple grapes. The fox licked his lips and jumped, but he could not reach them. He stepped back, took a running start, and jumped again, but the grapes swung just above his nose. He tried a third time and fell flat on his back in the dust. At last he stood up, brushed off his red fur, and walked away with his nose in the air. Those grapes are probably sour anyway, he said. A crow in a nearby tree laughed at him.
