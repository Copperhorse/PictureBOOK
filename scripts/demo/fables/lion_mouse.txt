A mighty lion was sleeping in the shade when a tiny mouse ran across his paw and woke him. The lion caught the mouse in his big paw and roared that he would eat her. Please let me go, squeaked the mouse, and one day I will help you. The lion laughed at the idea of a little mouse helping a king, but he let her go. A few days later, hunters came into the forest and trapped the lion in a strong rope net hung from a tree. He roared and roared, but he could not break free. The mouse heard him and came running. She climbed up the net and gnawed through the ropes with her sharp little teeth until the net fell open. The lion was free. Even a small friend can be a great friend, said the mouse.
