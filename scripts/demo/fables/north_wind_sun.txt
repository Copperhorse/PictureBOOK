The North Wind and the Sun argued about which of them was stronger. They saw a traveler walking down a road in a warm brown cloak. Let us see who can make him take off his cloak, said the Sun. The North Wind went first. He blew with all his might, and the cold gusts whirled around the man, but the traveler only pulled his cloak tighter. Then it was the Sun's turn. She shone gently and warmly from the sky. Soon the traveler was hot and wiped his forehead, and at last he took off his cloak and sat down in the shade of a tree. The North Wind had to admit that kindness is stronger than force.
