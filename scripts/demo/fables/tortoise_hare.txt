A hare was always bragging about how fast he could run. A slow old tortoise grew tired of the boasting and said, I will race you. The hare laughed so hard that he rolled on the grass, but he agreed. The animals of the forest gathered along the road to see the start. The fox raised a green flag, and the race began. The hare shot ahead and was soon out of sight, so he stopped under a big oak tree and said, I have plenty of time for a nap. The tortoise kept walking, step after slow step, and never stopped to rest. When the hare woke up, the sun was low, and he saw the tortoise just ahead of him at the finish line. He ran as fast as he could, but the tortoise crossed the line first, and the animals cheered. Slow and steady wins the race.
