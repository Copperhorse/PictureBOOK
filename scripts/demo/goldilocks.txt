Once upon a time, in a cosy little house at the edge of a green forest, there lived three bears. There was Papa Bear, who was big and gruff, Mama Bear, who was gentle and kind, and Baby Bear, who was small and very curious. One morning Mama Bear cooked porridge for breakfast and poured it into three bowls. The porridge was far too hot to eat, so the three bears decided to go for a walk in the forest while it cooled down.

Now, not far from there lived a little girl named Goldilocks, who had long golden curls and a bright red ribbon in her hair. Goldilocks was very adventurous, and she loved to wander where she should not. That very morning she skipped through the forest until she saw a little house with an open door. She knocked, but nobody answered, so she tiptoed inside.

In the kitchen she found a table with three bowls of porridge. She tasted the porridge from the great big bowl, but it was too hot. She tasted the porridge from the middle-sized bowl, but it was too cold. Then she tasted the porridge from the tiny little bowl, and it was just right, so she ate it all up.

Feeling full and sleepy, Goldilocks walked into the living room, where she saw three chairs. She sat in the great big chair, but it was too hard. She sat in the middle-sized chair, but it was too soft. Then she sat in the tiny little chair, and it was just right, but she was so heavy that the little chair broke into pieces and she tumbled onto the floor.

Goldilocks yawned and climbed the stairs to the bedroom. There she found three beds. She lay down on the great big bed, but it was too hard. She lay down on the middle-sized bed, but it was too soft. Then she lay down on the tiny little bed, and it was just right, and she fell fast asleep.

Soon the three bears came home from their walk, hungry for breakfast. Papa Bear looked at his bowl and said in a deep voice, somebody has been eating my porridge. Mama Bear looked at her bowl and said in a soft voice, somebody has been eating my porridge. Baby Bear looked at his bowl and cried in a squeaky voice, somebody has been eating my porridge, and it is all gone.

The three bears went into the living room and saw that their chairs had been moved. Baby Bear looked at his chair and cried, somebody has been sitting in my chair, and now it is broken. Then the three bears went up the stairs and found Goldilocks sleeping in Baby Bear's bed.

Baby Bear shouted, somebody has been sleeping in my bed, and here she is. Goldilocks woke up with a start. When she saw the three bears standing around her, she jumped out of the bed, ran down the stairs, and dashed out of the door. She ran all the way home through the forest and never went into the bears' house again.
