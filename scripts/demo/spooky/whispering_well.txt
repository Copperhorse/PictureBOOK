At the center of the village square stood an old stone well that nobody drank from anymore. Children said it whispered at night. One evening, a brave boy named Calder lowered the wooden bucket on its long rope to see what was down there. The rope went down, and down, and down, much farther than any well should be. Then the rope pulled tight, and something below began to pull back. Calder held on, and a faint voice drifted up from the darkness, saying, thank you, thank you, I am so cold. The rope grew wet and icy in his hands. He let go, and the rope slithered down the well like a snake and was gone. The bucket was found on the church steps the next morning, full of black water, and on the water floated a tiny golden ring.
