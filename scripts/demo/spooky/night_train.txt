Every midnight a black train stopped at the empty station in the little town of Hollow Creek, and nobody ever got on or off. One night a boy named Pip, who could not sleep, crept down to the platform with his lantern. The train doors slid open by themselves. Inside, rows of passengers sat very still in old-fashioned clothes, with their hands in their laps, and all of them turned their heads toward Pip at once. The conductor, tall and thin, in a long dark coat, held out a ticket and said, there is one seat left, and it is yours. Pip's lantern flickered. He took a step back, then another. The train whistle shrieked, and the doors began to close. Pip dropped the ticket, ran out of the station without looking back, and did not stop until he was in his bed. In the morning the train was gone, but a ticket lay on his pillow, with tomorrow's date on it.
