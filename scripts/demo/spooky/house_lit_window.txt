At the top of a hill stood an empty old house, and every night one window glowed with a yellow light. The children of the village said that nobody lived there. One stormy evening, a girl named Wren dared herself to go inside. The front door opened with a long creak, and the hall was cold and full of dust. She climbed the stairs, and the stairs groaned under each step. In the room with the glowing window, a candle burned on a table beside a second candle that was not lit. A cold hand touched her shoulder, and a pale lady in a long gray dress stood behind her, smiling sadly. I have been waiting a hundred years for someone to light my other candle, whispered the lady. Wren trembled, but she took a match and lit the second candle. The lady smiled, faded like mist, and the window went dark forever. Wren ran home through the rain, and the house on the hill was just an empty house.
