Three travelers, a tall merchant named Bram, a young nurse named Ada, and an old sailor named Quill, were caught in a snowstorm and knocked on the door of the Raven's Rest inn. The innkeeper, a pale man holding a candle, let them in and gave each of them a room on the second floor. The inn was silent, and every clock on the wall had stopped at midnight. In the dining room, a table was laid with steaming soup, but nobody else was there. Ada noticed that the portraits in the hall had their eyes turned to follow the travelers. Bram heard footsteps pacing above his bed all night. Quill found a cellar door that was slightly open, and a cold wind blew up from the dark stairs. At dawn the three met in the hall and agreed to leave at once. But when they opened the front door, the snow outside was gone, the fields were green, and the inn behind them was a broken ruin with no roof and no windows. The innkeeper stood in the doorway, smiling, and said, you are welcome to stay, as everyone who comes here does. Then he blew out his candle. The travelers ran across the green fields without looking back, and none of them ever spoke of that night again.
