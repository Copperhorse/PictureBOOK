Under the dusty roof of her grandmother's house, a girl named Isla found an old mirror with a cracked golden frame. She waved at her reflection, and her reflection waved back. She stuck out her tongue, and the reflection did the same. Then Isla turned away to leave, and from behind her came a soft tap on the glass. She looked back, and the girl in the mirror was not copying her anymore. The reflection pressed her pale hands against the glass and mouthed, let me out. Isla stepped backward, but the other girl smiled, and her smile was full of tiny sharp teeth. The glass rippled like water, and a hand came out of the mirror. Isla grabbed an old blanket and threw it over the mirror, and the tapping slowly stopped. She locked the attic door and never went up there again, but every night she could hear a faint tapping from above.
