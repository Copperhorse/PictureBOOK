Old Harlow kept the lighthouse at the end of the rocky point, and every night he climbed the spiral stairs to light the great lamp. One foggy night, he saw a ship with torn gray sails drifting toward the rocks, and it made no sound at all. Harlow rang the bell and waved his lantern, but the ship did not turn. Its deck was empty, and a pale green light glowed in every porthole. As the ship passed close beneath the tower, he saw a row of faces pressed against the glass, and every one of them was looking up at him. They all raised their hands together and waved. The ship slid into the fog and vanished. In the morning the sea was calm and the rocks were empty, but there was a wet footprint on every step of the spiral stairs, climbing up to the lamp room.
