Mira was a little girl with round glasses and a bright yellow raincoat. One windy morning she carried her red kite to the top of the green hill behind her house. The wind pulled the kite high into the sky, and Mira laughed and laughed. Then the string snapped, and the kite flew away over the river. Mira ran down to the riverbank, where a kind old fisherman had caught it in his net, and he gave it back with a smile.
